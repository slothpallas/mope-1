MOPE-1

It's a synthesizer. It's having a hard time.

Free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: a sad wavetable synth you play through a blob that lives in a small, rainy room. Whatever you turn happens to the blob.

Play it in the browser Put it on your FM-1

What it is

  • A wavetable synth with sixteen wavetables, six voices of two oscillators each, five kinds of filter, two LFOs, a delay and a reverb.
  • A backing band in the style of an old toy keyboard, except that every beat it knows is a sad one (Grief Drill, Funeral Bossa, Office Ambivalent and ten others). A bass follows your chords, and there's an arpeggiator and a loop you can fix one step at a time.
  • It runs on the M-VAVE FM-1, it's free and open source (GPL-3.0), and you can always put the original firmware back.

Try it, then put it on

Play it in the browser

The player runs the same firmware, compiled for the browser, on a drawing of the FM-1. You can play it from your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard, and there's nothing to install. Open the player

Put it on your FM-1 (it won't mind)

  • First download M-VAVE's own firmware (FM-1 V15, "PC Firmware", from m-vave.com/download), so you can always go back.
  • Plug the FM-1 straight into a computer with a USB cable that carries data (no hub). Close other apps that use MIDI. Use Chrome or Edge, and allow MIDI with SysEx when it asks.
  • Open the installer and press Install MOPE-1. It takes about a minute: leave the page open and don't unplug it. If it gets cut off, press Install again: it carries on. The FM-1 restarts as MOPE-1.
  • To leave, use the installer's way back to M-VAVE's firmware (with the V15 file you kept), or install another firmware the same way.

The manual

There are nine buttons with four knobs each, and that's most of it.

  1. Press a part's button.
  2. Turn its four knobs.
  3. Press the button again for its second page.

The page opens in a drawer under the blob and slides away three seconds after you stop, but it stays selected: the button stays lit and the knobs keep turning that part. Press a button twice quickly to keep the drawer open until you press HOME.

The rest of the panel

  • PRESETS turns through 38 whole sounds. ALGORITHM changes only the wavetable. SELECT changes the band's tape.
  • PLAY starts the band. REC records what you play into the loop. SAVE keeps everything (hold it for the factory settings).
  • OCT− and OCT+ move the keys. A MIDI keyboard works too, over USB.
  • The README has every setting with its range, if you want the details.

Will it break my FM-1?
It has been installed on a real FM-1 and taken off again many times. If a firmware crashes at start-up, the FM-1 comes up in safe mode after two tries, and the installer still works there. The chip's own update mode can't be overwritten. Custom firmware is still at your own risk, though.
Can I go back?
Yes. The installer can put M-VAVE's V15 back, or you can install another firmware the same way. Saved projects don't carry over: MOPE-1 can't read other firmwares' saves, and they can't read its.
