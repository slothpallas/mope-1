MOPE-1

It's a synthesizer. It's having a hard time.

Switch it on, then play it from your computer keys or a MIDI keyboard. The full player

What it is

  • Free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1Open source (GPL-3.0). The original firmware goes back on any time.
  • A blob is the interfaceWhatever you turn happens to it: its eyes, its mouth, its tears, its room.
  • A sad wavetable synthTwo oscillators a voice, five kinds of filter, two LFOs, a delay and a reverb.
  • A backing band that only plays sad beatsLike an old toy keyboard's: Grief Drill, Funeral Bossa, Office Ambivalent. A bass follows your chords.
  • A loop and an arpeggiatorRecord what you play, then fix it one step at a time.

Put it on your FM-1

  1. 1Download M-VAVE's firmware first (FM-1 V15, "PC Firmware", m-vave.com), to go back later.
  2. 2Plug the FM-1 straight into a computer with a USB data cable. No hub. Close other MIDI apps.
  3. 3In Chrome or Edge, open the installer and allow MIDI with SysEx.
  4. 4Press Install MOPE-1. It takes about a minute. If it's cut off, press it again.
Open the installer

To go back, the installer puts M-VAVE's V15 back. Saved projects don't carry over between firmwares.

The manual

  1. 1Press a part's button.
  2. 2Turn its four knobs.
  3. 3Press the button again for its second page.

The page opens in a drawer under the blob and slides away three seconds after you stop, but it stays selected: the knobs keep turning that part. Press a button twice quickly to keep the drawer open until HOME.

Recording and deleting a loop

RECWhile stopped: a bar's count-in, then it records. While playing: it records on top.
RECAgain: it stops recording. The loop keeps playing.
HOME+RECUndo the last take.
SAVE+RECDelete the whole loop.
SEQthentap a white keyDelete that step's notes. The 16 white keys are the bar's steps.
SEQthenpress a stepTurn NOTE, LEN and VEL to change it. OCT− OCT+ go to the other bars.
hold SAVEthenSAVEHold 1.5 s, press again: everything back to the start (sound, settings, loop).

Will it break my FM-1?
It has been installed on a real one and taken off again many times. If a firmware crashes at start-up, the FM-1 comes up in safe mode, and the installer still works there. Still at your own risk.
Can I go back?
Yes: the installer puts M-VAVE's V15 back, or install another firmware the same way.
