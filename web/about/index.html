MOPE-1

It's a synthesizer. It's having a hard time.

Free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. A sad wavetable synth whose interface is a blob in a rainy room. Everything you turn happens to it.

Play it in the browser Put it on your FM-1

What it is.

  • A wavetable synth. Sixteen wavetables, two oscillators a voice, six voices, five kinds of filter, two LFOs, a room and a delay.
  • A backing band, like an old toy keyboard's, that only knows depressing beats. Grief Drill. Funeral Bossa. Office Ambivalent. There's a bass that follows you, an arpeggiator and a loop you can fix step by step.
  • It runs on the M-VAVE FM-1. It's free and open source (GPL-3.0). You can always go back.

Try it. Then put it on.

Play it in the browser.

The same firmware, compiled for the browser, on a drawing of the FM-1. Your computer's keys play it, and it answers a MIDI keyboard. Nothing to install. Open the player

Put it on your keyboard. It won't mind.

  • First download M-VAVE's own firmware (FM-1 V15, "PC Firmware", from m-vave.com/download), so you can always go back.
  • Plug the FM-1 straight into a computer with a USB cable that carries data (no hub). Close other apps that use MIDI. Use Chrome or Edge, and allow MIDI with SysEx when it asks.
  • Open the installer and press Install MOPE-1. It takes about a minute: leave the page open and don't unplug it. If it gets cut off, press Install again: it carries on. The FM-1 restarts as MOPE-1.
  • To leave, the installer has a way back to M-VAVE's firmware (the V15 file you kept). Or install another firmware the same way. It doesn't mind that either.

The manual.

Nine buttons. Four knobs each. Nobody needs more than that.

  1. Press a part.
  2. Turn its four knobs.
  3. Press it again for its other side.

The part's page opens in a drawer under the blob and closes three seconds after you stop. The page stays: its button stays lit and the knobs still turn it. Press a button twice quickly and the drawer stays open until HOME.

The rest of the panel.

  • PRESETS turns through 38 whole sounds. ALGORITHM changes only the wavetable. SELECT changes the band's tape.
  • PLAY starts the band. REC records what you play into the loop. SAVE keeps everything (hold it for the factory settings).
  • OCT− and OCT+ move the keys. A MIDI keyboard works too, over USB.
  • The numbers are in the README, if you want them.

Will it break my FM-1?
It has been installed and taken off a real one many times. If a firmware ever crashes at start-up, the FM-1 comes up in a safe mode after two tries, with the installer still working. The chip's own update mode can't be overwritten. Custom firmware is still at your own risk.
Can I go back?
Yes. The installer puts M-VAVE's V15 back, or install another firmware the same way. MOPE-1 can't read other firmwares' saved projects, and they can't read its.
