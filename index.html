MOPE-1

a sad wavetable synth for the M-VAVE FM-1. It plays anyway. Running in the browser from the same C code.

Turn your phone sideways for bigger controls.

Off. Sound starts when you switch it on.

Install on your FM-1

Beta. Custom firmware is installed at your own risk. Before you start, download M-VAVE's stock firmware (FM-1 V15, "PC Firmware" at m-vave.com/download) so you can always go back (below).

  1. Connect the FM-1 to this computer with a USB data cable, directly (no hub, probe or Transporter). Close other apps that use MIDI.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge, and allow MIDI (with SysEx) when the browser asks.
  3. Press Install. Don't unplug or close this page while it writes (about a minute). The FM-1 restarts into MOPE-1.

Back to the stock firmware

This writes M-VAVE's own FM-1 V15 back, from MOPE-1 (or its safe mode, or any Felucca-family firmware) over the same USB cable. Choose the file you downloaded from M-VAVE: it is checked (SHA-256) before anything is written. If the FM-1 doesn't start at all, it comes up in safe mode after two tries, where this still works; past that, tools/fm1_rescue.py in the repository restores it from a Mac.

It is the sound

    
    
      
  • There are no menus and no numbers. The blob is the synth: its outline is the wavetable, its eyes are the filter, its mouth is the envelope, its tears are the delay.
    • 
      
  • Press or hold a part's button: its schematic opens under the blob (the envelope's shape, the LFO's wave, the filter's curve, the wavetable's frames, the echoes, the room's tail, the drum grid, the mixer) and the four knobs turn that part. It stays 3 s after your last touch. Press a button twice quickly and its page stays until HOME.
    • 
      
  • HOME Body: table, position, detune (a ghost of itself), sub (heavier).
    • 
      
  • EDIT Eyes: cutoff (closed: stoned), resonance (pinpoint pupils, then lasers), envelope amount and mod decay (the eyebrows).
    • 
      
  • ENV Mouth: attack, decay, sustain, release (a long one is a yawn).
    • 
      
  • LFO Vibe: rate, depth, shape (sway, bob, bounce, nod, twitch), where it goes (shape, eyes, pitch, volume).
    • 
      
  • FX Tears: chorus (more of it, out of step), delay (one tear an echo), time (how far apart they fall), feedback (how many).
    • 
      
  • SEL Walls: reverb (waves bounce off the alley), size (the walls go out, the blob gets smaller; then the void), fry, tape.
    • 
      
  • SEQ Tape deck: style, tempo, swing, human. GLO Mixer: voice, drums, bass, gunk (the room's rain and crackle, louder when idle); GLO again: Setup (glide, MIDI channel, MIDI out, tune). ARP held: the arpeggiator's mode, rate, octaves, gate; tapped: on / off.
    • 
    

  

  

    
Playing

    
    
      
  • The 27 keys play F3 to G5. OCT− / OCT+ move them.
    • 
      
  • ALGORITHM changes only the sound source, the wavetable (its body). PRESETS loads a whole sound at once (wave, filter, envelope, LFO, effects) and shows its card: 21 of them in six kinds, pad, keys, pluck, lead, bass and FX. SELECT the drum tape: the blob swaps the cassette and shrugs.
    • 
      
  • PLAY the drums: grief drill, empty city pop, gloom gabber, missed phonk call, jersey cry club, heartbreakcore, amapia-no, cold footwork, dembow out, vaporweep, hyperflop, funeral bossa, office ambivalent. Kicks squash it, snares make it blink, hats are rain.
    • 
      
  • ARP the arpeggiator: hold a chord and it plays it note by note at the tempo (up, down, up and down, wander, stab), with the drums. The auto-bass plays under the drums whenever the mixer's Bass is up, always in a minor key: hold C and it plays A minor (all notes of C major); hold a minor chord and it plays that minor. REC hold: the keys stay down.
    • 
      
  • Six lights over its head are its voices. Chorus: see-through copies of it peek out from behind. On the Eyes and Mouth pages the view moves in close. More notes than voices: a light gets yeeted. Too loud: it catches fire, calmly. Leave it alone: it sleeps; leave it longer: it stares. Every note is XP. Levels don't do anything.
    • 
      
  • Hold SAVE for 1.5 s to forget everything (SAVE again to confirm). It still remembers its level.
    • 
    

  

  

    
Mouse, touch and keyboard

    
    
      
  • Drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it. Click or touch keys and buttons; several fingers work on a touch screen.
    • 
      
  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–11: 1–0, -.
    • 
      
  • Space drums, Enter hold, B arpeggiator, Esc body, Z / X octave, ↑ ↓ instrument (ALGORITHM), ← → drum tape (SELECT).
    • 
      
  • Shift-click (or right-click) a button to hold it down.
    • 
    

  

  

    
MIDI

    
Connect MIDI to play it from a keyboard: notes at their velocity, pitch bend, the mod wheel moves the table's position, program change picks a mood, CC 74 cutoff, 71 resonance, 73 attack, 72 release, 70 position, 91 reverb, 93 chorus, 94 delay, 7 volume.

  

  

    
What this is

    
The firmware's UI and sound engine compiled to WebAssembly at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz. A fork of FiMba-1 (GPL-3.0), itself from FoMni, X0X and Felucca: their boot, update loader, safe mode, plate reverb and this emulator are theirs.
