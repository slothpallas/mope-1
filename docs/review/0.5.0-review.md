# MOPE-1 0.5.0: the second round of AI reviews, and what was done about it

The same four AI reviewers (a hardware-synth sound designer, an experimental sound designer, a producer,
a live keyboardist) looked again after the macros, the new effects and the new presets went in. As in
the first round they could not listen: they judged from host renders, the settings and screenshots.

## What they found still wrong

1. **The macros moved loudness more than tone.** EYES up to 32 dB (PICK A FIGHT), BITE -15..+11 dB.
2. **EYES ran backwards or did nothing** on the other filter kinds (high-pass, vowel, comb).
3. **Two new presets were badly made:** SMALL TALK had no bass (9% under 120 Hz); PICK A FIGHT's sync
   could not sweep (nothing could move the shadow oscillator's pitch).
4. **Too many presets sat still** (LFO 2 off in 42 of 65), and the new effects were never an LFO's target.
5. **Levels and bass width:** 7.2 dB between presets; three basses too wide for one speaker.
6. **FX's two-letter tabs** were a code to learn; the macro dials were small.

## What was done (measured on host renders: tools/presets)

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| EYES: change of the top end, 0 -> 100 | some backwards, some ~0 | +10 .. +50 dB on every preset (median 34) |
| EYES: change of loudness | up to 32 dB | -7.5 .. +5.6 dB (median -2.7) |
| BITE: change of loudness | -15 .. +11 dB | -7.6 .. +5.9 dB (median -1.0) |
| loudness between presets | 7.2 dB | 0.5 dB |
| bass level between the basses | 13.3 dB | 7.3 dB |
| presets with LFO 2 off | 42 | 26 |
| SMALL TALK under 120 Hz | 9% | 94% |
| PICK A FIGHT brightness moving while held | 8% | 21% |

- EYES shuts a low-pass to ~250 Hz (muffled, not gone) and gives back most of the level it takes; the
  other filter kinds move +-1 octave (a high-pass 2) with a tilt after the filter, so up is always brighter.
- Fry and Burn now even their own level out as heard (what goes in and comes out, measured).
- New LFO destination: the shadow's pitch (+-an octave).
- The voices' lows (under ~150 Hz) are mono.
- Tabs show whole names, as many as fit around the open page, with arrows where more hide.

## Not done

- Preset order and a jump by kind (the owner chose to keep the order).
- Velocity (the FM-1's keys have none), tempo-synced or key-retriggered LFOs, kick ducking, a noise source.
- Some presets still barely move while held (31 of 65 under 15%), mostly plucks, sub basses and pads
  meant to be steady; NERVES adds movement to any of them.
- Every level is from host renders; the FM-1 measurements (fm1_levels.py, fm1_load.py with the band)
  are still to do.
