# MOPE-1 0.4.1: four AI reviews of the factory sounds

Four AI reviewers (a hardware-synth sound designer, an experimental sound designer, a producer, a live
keyboardist) judged the 54 factory presets from their settings and from measurements of host renders
(a held chord, a riff, a bass line, and a riff over the band). **None of them could listen.** The
numbers are in `build/review/presets.md` (made by rendering every preset with `build/host/mope_host`).

## What all four agree on

1. **The bank is dark.** 42 of 54 presets have under 5% of their energy above 2 kHz; the median
   brightness of a held chord is 322 Hz. Leads that should cut measure like filtered sines
   (DRAMA 211 Hz, LOW BATTERY 250 Hz, BENT OUT OF SHAPE 323 Hz). Only DEEP FRIED is properly bright.
2. **Held notes barely move.** Only 5 presets change brightness by more than 30% while held.
   LFO 2 is off in 38 of 54, both LFOs in 8; 29 of the active LFOs are plain sines. The engine's best
   tricks are almost unused: table scan in 3 presets, effect amounts in 4, cross-modulation (FM,
   sync, ring) in 15, the high-pass filter in none.
3. **Leads, keys and basses are not tight.** REPLY ALL takes 810 ms to reach its level, ALL CAPS 450,
   LEFT ON READ (keys) 370. The plucks are excellent (2-3 ms).
4. **Levels and bass weight are uneven.** Held level spreads 9.4 dB (PASSIVE AGGRESSIVE -12.4 dB,
   SAYS NOTHING -21.8 dB); the basses 7.5 dB (RAGE QUIT to BURNOUT). Three basses are too wide for
   mono (RAGE QUIT 0.77, BURNOUT 0.40, BOTTOM TEXT 0.38).
5. **The order hides the good ones.** Presets 0-8 are all pads; kinds are scattered; 54 + 16 sounds
   on one knob with no jump by kind.
6. **The macros are the most important planned thing** (all four), if every preset is tuned so each
   goes from tame to extreme.

Verdicts: "a tool with a toy's preset bank" (producer); "uses perhaps a quarter of the engine"
(experimental designer); "undersells the engine" (hardware designer); "a real instrument that plays
like a mood piece; the blob is not the problem, the patches and the reach are" (keyboardist).

## Checked against the audio

- **"Tails everywhere" is mostly a noise floor, not tails.** Many presets never fall below about
  -35 dB after the keys are up, and it does not decay (DOOMSCROLL: -36 dB half a second after, -35 dB
  two seconds after). Presets with Fry sit about 11 dB higher than those without (median -33 dB
  against -44 dB). The room's rain is meant to be there; Fry lifting it is not. To find and fix.
- **Dark: confirmed**, also on the higher riff (median brightness: basses 313 Hz, keys 460 Hz,
  leads 867 Hz). The test chord is low (C2-Eb3), which makes the figure a little worse than playing
  higher would.

## Presets

- **Strongest (named by two or more):** DEEP FRIED, MAIN CHARACTER, PASSIVE AGGRESSIVE, 3AM,
  SEEN 2:14, NPC, DOOMSCROLL, LANDLORD.
- **Rework (two or more):** DRAMA, LOW BATTERY, REPLY ALL, ALL CAPS, LEFT ON READ, RAGE QUIT,
  BURNOUT, DOOMSCROLL (no squelch), BENT OUT OF SHAPE, BUBBLE WRAP, SCREAM INTO PILLOW.
- **Drop (two or more):** HOLD MUSIC, WEIGHTED BLANKET, TINNITUS, GHOSTED, HEAVY BREATHING,
  SUNDAY SCARIES, THE VOID STARES, ELEVATOR, SLOW HEART.

## Missing sounds (named by two or more)

Bright saw / brass stab; mono reese bass; 808-style sub with glide; dry mono lead with glide;
snarling sync lead; FM / ring bells and metal; a Fizmo-style wave sequence (two LFOs on table and
position at unrelated rates); comb plucked string and rhythmic comb; talking (vowel) bass;
high-pass glass.

## On the plan

- **Macros:** yes, first. ROOM needs a true zero (bone dry); EYES must reach properly bright.
- **Tabs:** yes; make them big and bright.
- **Browsing by kind** (not in the plan): three of four rank it above any new effect.
- **New effects:** BURN overlaps Drive and Fry: make it a different character (after the filter, or
  a wavefolder). SWIRL: fine, second. MUMBLE: the keys have no velocity, so it should follow the
  beat or an LFO first.
- **Engine wishes (two or more):** the shadow oscillator's pitch as a destination for the Sigh and
  the LFOs (sync can't sweep without it); LFOs that follow the tempo and restart on a key; a
  drive after the filter. One each: kick ducking; a noise source.
- **3D wavetable:** the keyboardist calls it decoration. It stays (the owner asked for it), after
  the sound work.
